# GitHub-style contribution hover details

## What will change
- Add a GitHub-style tooltip to every contribution square showing the exact count and a readable date, such as “19 contributions on October 3rd.”
- Give hovered and keyboard-focused squares a subtle lift, outline, and glow so the active day is easy to track.
- Style the tooltip as warm translucent glass in the colorful theme and crisp black-and-white in minimal mode.
- Keep the current contribution data, year totals, legend, labels, and mobile horizontal scrolling unchanged.

## Interaction details
- Tooltips appear after a short hover pause and fade/slide smoothly.
- Contribution squares remain accessible by keyboard and expose the same detail to assistive technology.
- Motion is reduced or removed when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

## Verification
- Check the Classic page on desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm the tooltip text uses correct singular/plural grammar and ordinal dates.
- Confirm both colorful and monochrome themes render cleanly without clipping or overlap.
